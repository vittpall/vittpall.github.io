Publications

  • DynGhost: Temporally-Modelled Transformer for Dynamic Ghost Imaging with Quantum Detectors
    V. Palladino, A. E. Cetin
    arXiv:2605.10185 · 2026 · Accepted at MMSP 2026
    A transformer with joint spatial and temporal attention for ghost imaging of dynamic scenes, trained with a quantum-aware framework that models Poisson single-photon detector statistics. arXiv
  • EnergyLens: Interpretable Closed-Form Energy Models for Multimodal LLM Inference Serving
    V. Palladino, G. Palermo, M. E. Papka, Z. Lan
    arXiv:2605.10556 · 2026 · Submitted to SC 2026
    Symbolic regression over profiling data yields a twelve-parameter closed-form energy model that picks the optimal serving configuration 88.2% of the time using ~50 profiling runs. arXiv
  • WTHaar-Net: a Hybrid Quantum-Classical Approach
    V. Palladino, T. Idden, A. E. Cetin
    arXiv:2603.02497 · 2026 · Submitted to ECCV
    Hybrid quantum-classical CNN using Haar Wavelet Transform, achieving parameter reduction with competitive accuracy on CIFAR-10 and Tiny-ImageNet; validated on IBM Quantum hardware.
